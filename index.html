THE IDEA

Sauna in Ely.
We are exploring it.

The concept is simple: an outdoor sauna on the Adventure Inn property in Ely, designed for both locals and visitors. Warm up after the trail, the lake, the snow, or just a long week.

Ely Sauna would remain its own experience. You would not necessarily need to be staying at the hotel to use it. Exact access, hours and packages are still being shaped.

A REAL PLACE

Not an imaginary lake resort.

The proposed sauna would live on an existing property in Ely, alongside the outdoor gathering space already there. The goal is to create something useful, relaxed and genuinely local rather than an overbuilt spa concept.

FAQ

A few things to know.

Is Ely Sauna open already?

No. This page is intentionally being used to measure interest before a final decision is made.

Where would it be?

The concept is for an outdoor sauna on the Adventure Inn property in Ely, Minnesota.

Would it only be for hotel guests?

No final access model has been set. We are specifically measuring interest from locals, seasonal residents and visitors as well as hotel guests.

What kind of sauna?

We are evaluating an outdoor cedar sauna experience. The exact unit, capacity and layout are not final. Images on this site show the direction we are considering.

When could it open?

Timing depends on interest, final planning and installation. Joining the list is the best way to hear what happens next.
